1. ZERO ADVERTISING TRACKERS
Downex does not contain Google Analytics, Facebook Pixels, telemetry SDKs, or
advertising identifiers. We believe your software should be a quiet utility,
not a tracking billboard.

2. ZERO DATA HARVESTING
We do not collect, store, or sell:
- The URLs or files you download.
- The websites you visit.
- Your personal IP address or location history.
- Your file names or system directories.

3. LOCAL STORAGE IS YOURS
All download records, queues, and settings stay 100% on your local hard drive.
When you delete a download record or clear your history in Downex, it is deleted
from your disk completely.

Gaurav Patil stands firmly behind the principle that privacy is a fundamental
human right in software.
