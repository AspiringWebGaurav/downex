1. CRYPTOGRAPHIC SIGNING
Every release of Downex is signed with cryptographic keys (Ed25519) before it
leaves our build servers. Your local Downex installation verifies these
cryptographic signatures before accepting any update. If an update file has been
altered by even one byte, it is rejected instantly.

2. MEMORY AND LOCAL STORAGE PROTECTION
Downex saves your download history and queue preferences in a secure, local
database stored on your machine. We never upload your download list, links, or
saved paths to the cloud.

3. SAFEGUARD AGAINST ROGUE DOWNLOADS
Downex inspects incoming files and helps you avoid accidental execution of
hidden scripts by displaying the verified final file extension clearly before
you click to open.
