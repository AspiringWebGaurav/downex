1. ANTI-TAMPERING MONITORING
Downex employs continuous runtime integrity verification. The application
monitors its own binary headers, in-memory execution boundaries, and
cryptographic trust chains to prevent malicious third parties from injecting
spyware, malware, or keyloggers into Downex distribution copies.

2. STRICT PROHIBITION ON MODIFICATION & CRACKING
Any attempt to:
- Decompile, patch, or reverse engineer the binary for unauthorized distribution;
- Strip creator attribution or replace licensing tokens;
- Intercept and spoof the cryptographic update channel;
- Inject runtime DLLs or modify memory structures;
is strictly prohibited and violates both this agreement and international
intellectual property laws.

3. HARDWARE-LEVEL MACHINE BAN ENFORCEMENT
To protect our users and infrastructure against cracked or poisoned software
variants, Downex is integrated with automated hardware device verification.

Any machine detected attempting to tamper with internal binaries, spoof release
signatures, or distribute unauthorized modified builds will be permanently
blacklisted via a hardware-level machine identifier (derived from Motherboard
UUID and CPU serial hashes).

Once blacklisted:
- The machine is permanently barred from accessing the update network.
- All future OTA updates and cloud synchronization services are refused.
- Automated recovery systems will quarantine the modified installation.
