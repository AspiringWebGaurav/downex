THANK YOU FOR CHOOSING DOWNEX!

Hello and welcome! Thank you for downloading and installing Downex.

I built Downex out of personal frustration with the state of modern software.
For years, whenever I needed to download large files, videos, software builds, or
media, I was forced to use download managers that felt like relics from the past—
ugly interfaces, annoying popups asking for money every five minutes, confusing
settings menus, and worst of all, sneaky toolbars and advertisements bundled in.

I believed that Windows users deserved something dramatically better:
- An app that is clean, quiet, and beautiful on your desktop.
- An app that respects your computer's memory and battery life.
- An app that downloads files as fast as your internet connection physically allows.
- An app that never pesters you with patch notes, upgrade popups, or ads.

That is why Downex exists. It is completely independent, carefully engineered,
and built from scratch to give you the most effortless download experience
possible on Windows.

I truly hope Downex makes your daily computer use faster and more enjoyable.

QUICK START — HOW TO USE DOWNEX
================================================================================

Downex is designed so that you can start using it immediately without reading a
huge manual, but here are a few handy tips to get the most out of it:

1. STARTING A NEW DOWNLOAD (Ctrl + N)
   - Simply copy any download link from your web browser.
   - Switch to Downex and press Ctrl+N (or click the "+ New Download" button).
   - Downex will automatically detect the link on your clipboard, check the file
     name and size, and let you choose where to save it with a single click.

2. LIGHTNING SEARCH (Ctrl + K)
   - Have dozens of downloaded files and need to find something fast?
   - Press Ctrl+K (or the '/' key) from anywhere in the app.
   - Start typing the file name, extension (.zip, .mp4, .iso), or date.
   - Downex filters your list instantly with zero lag.

3. SMART QUEUES & ORGANIZED DOWNLOADS
   - Use the Queues view on the left sidebar to prioritize what downloads first.
   - You can pause individual downloads, reorder items, or let Downex download
     files one after another so your internet stays responsive for gaming or calls.

4. COMPACT MODE (F8)
   - Need more screen space while multitasking?
   - Press F8 (or click the collapse arrow at the top of the sidebar) to tuck
     the navigation sidebar into a slim, minimal icon bar.

HANDY KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS
================================================================================

Keyboard power users can control almost everything in Downex without touching
the mouse:

- Ctrl + N         Open the New Download modal
- Ctrl + K         Focus the universal search bar
- /                Quick-search shortcut (when not typing in a text field)
- F8               Toggle sidebar between full width and compact mode
- Escape           Dismiss any open dialog or modal
- Ctrl + Q         Close the main window to the Windows System Tray


================================================================================
CLEAN & RESPECTFUL DESIGN PROMISES
================================================================================

1. ZERO ADVERTISEMENTS & ZERO TRACKING
   Downex will never show you banner ads, sponsored popups, or recommended
   bloatware. Your download links and personal browsing habits are strictly your
   own business. We never track, log, or sell your data.

2. UNDER 15 MB RAM IN BACKGROUND
   When Downex is minimized to your system tray waiting for links, it goes into a
   deep, peaceful sleep mode. It consumes less than 15 MB of RAM and 0% CPU, so
   your laptop battery lasts just as long as if the app wasn't even there.

3. SILENT & INVISIBLE UPDATES
   When a new version of Downex is released with improvements, Downex updates
   itself quietly in the background without interrupting your work. You will
   never be assaulted by annoying "What's New in Version X.Y.Z" modal popups or
   forced reboots. It just gets better over time.

4. RESPECTFUL UNINSTALLER
   If you ever decide to uninstall Downex, the uninstaller will politely and
   silently remove the app, shortcuts, and background files. It will NEVER open
   your browser to ask you survey questions ("Why did you leave us?"). When you
   say remove, it removes cleanly.
