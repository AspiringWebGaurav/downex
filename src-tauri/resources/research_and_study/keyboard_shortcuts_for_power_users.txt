Every time your hand has to leave the keyboard to reach for the mouse, find a
cursor, and click a button, your mental flow is broken by a fraction of a second.

For power users, keyboard shortcuts are not a secondary feature; they are the
primary way of interacting with software.

Downex is built with full keyboard navigation:
- Ctrl + N: Instantly paste and start a new download without clicking anywhere.
- Ctrl + K: Search through hundreds of past downloads with instantaneous filtering.
- F8: Collapse the sidebar to reclaim screen space.
- Escape: Dismiss any active modal or return to the main list.

By streamlining common actions into muscle-memory shortcuts, Downex lets you
manage dozens of downloads in seconds.
