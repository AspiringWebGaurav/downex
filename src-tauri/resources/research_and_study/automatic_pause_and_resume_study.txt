Title: HOW AUTOMATIC PAUSE AND RESUME SAVES YOUR TIME
Author & Architect: Gaurav Patil (https://gauravpatil.site)

There is nothing more frustrating than downloading a 10 GB file, reaching 95%,
and then having your internet disconnect for two seconds—only for the browser
to say "Download Failed" and make you start over from 0%!

Why did that happen? Because traditional browsers often treat a download as a
single continuous stream. If the stream breaks, they don't know where they left
off.

Downex is built around byte-level tracking:
- Every byte written to your drive is tracked with a precise offset index.
- If your laptop goes to sleep, your Wi-Fi drops, or you click Pause to join a
  Zoom meeting, Downex remembers the exact byte count saved.
- When you reconnect or click Resume, Downex sends a "Range: bytes=X-" header to
  the server.
- The download resumes instantly from the exact millisecond it paused!

You never lose progress, and you never waste your time or bandwidth.
