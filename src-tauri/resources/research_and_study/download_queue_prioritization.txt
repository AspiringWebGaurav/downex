When you need to download twenty files at once—such as a series of work assets,
video lectures, or project libraries—downloading all 20 simultaneously can choke
your connection.

When 20 files fight for bandwidth at the same time:
- None of them finish quickly.
- Every file crawls along at 5% progress.
- If your computer needs to shut down, you are left with twenty half-finished files
  instead of ten completed ones.

Downex uses Smart Queue Prioritization:
- You can tell Downex to download 2 or 3 files at a time.
- The highest-priority downloads get 100% of your bandwidth and finish rapidly.
- As each download completes, Downex automatically moves to the next file in line.

Get the files you need now, and let the rest finish smoothly in the background.
