Title: DESIGN ERGONOMICS: LIGHT & DARK MODES

Color in software is not just an aesthetic choice; it is an ergonomic one.

When you are working in a brightly lit office, a high-contrast Light Theme with
crisp white backgrounds and deep zinc typography reduces eye strain and makes
text effortlessly legible.

Conversely, when you are downloading files late at night in a dim room, a blinding
white screen causes pupil constriction and visual fatigue. A carefully balanced
Dark Theme—using deep zinc (#18181B) rather than pitch black, paired with soft
gray text and emerald accents—protects your eyes and feels relaxing.

Downex is 100% theme-aware:
- You can switch between Light and Dark mode with a single click.
- Even the standalone updater window dynamically adapts to your theme, matching
  your chosen palette so your desktop always looks unified.
