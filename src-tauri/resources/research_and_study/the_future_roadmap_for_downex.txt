Downex is already a fast, reliable, and respectful download manager, but the
journey of craftsmanship never truly ends.

Here is what Gaurav Patil is exploring for upcoming releases:
- Mobile Companion Link: Seamlessly hand off download links between your Android
  device and your Windows PC.
- Torrent and Peer-to-Peer Protocol Support: Bringing Downex's clean, bloat-free
  philosophy to distributed downloads.
- Automated Cloud Mirroring: Option to automatically sync completed downloads to
  your personal private cloud storage.
- Deeper Browser Integration: One-click video stream capture from popular media
  platforms.

Thank you for being part of this journey.
Downex will always stay fast, clean, private, and bloat-free.
