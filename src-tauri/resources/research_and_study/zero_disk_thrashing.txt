Solid State Drives (SSDs) are fast, but they have a finite lifespan measured
in write endurance (Terabytes Written, or TBW).

Badly designed download managers cause "disk thrashing"—they write tiny 4-kilobyte
chunks randomly across the drive, causing the SSD controller to perform costly
block erase cycles that wear out the drive prematurely.

Downex protects your SSD through Sequential Buffered I/O:
- Instead of writing every incoming packet to the disk instantly, Downex collects
  data into a 512 KB memory buffer.
- Once full, it writes the entire block sequentially into its exact file location
  in a single disk operation.
- This maximizes disk throughput, prevents drive stutter, and significantly
  reduces wear on your SSD over the lifetime of your computer.

Craftsmanship means caring about the physical hardware your software runs on.
