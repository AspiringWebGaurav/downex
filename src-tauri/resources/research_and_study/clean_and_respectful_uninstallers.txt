You can tell everything about a software company by how they behave when you
decide to leave.

Bad software acts like a desperate salesman:
- It hides the uninstaller deep in obscure subfolders.
- It displays three different confirmation dialogs ("Are you SURE you want to leave?").
- When you click Yes, it forces open your web browser to a 10-question survey page.
- It leaves behind registry keys, cache folders, and background services that
  continue running even after you thought you removed it.

Downex follows the Clean Uninstaller Protocol:
- The uninstaller (uninstall.exe) is right there in the installation folder.
- When run, it politely and silently closes any background processes.
- It removes all application files, shortcuts, and temporary caches.
- It NEVER opens your web browser. ZERO surveys. ZERO spam.

We earn your trust by respecting your choices from the day you install to the day
you uninstall.
