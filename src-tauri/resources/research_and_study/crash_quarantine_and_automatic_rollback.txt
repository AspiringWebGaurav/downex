What happens if a sudden power outage or blue screen strikes your computer right
in the middle of updating a program?

In most traditional software, you are left with a broken, corrupted binary that
crashes every time you try to open it. The user has to manually go to a website,
download a fresh installer, and reinstall from scratch.

Downex is built with an autonomous emergency recovery engine:
- If a newly updated binary crashes twice before establishing a healthy runtime
  heartbeat, the independent sentinel (DownexRecovery.exe) springs into action
  with zero human intervention needed.
- It quarantines the failing binary (marking it .bad).
- It atomically restores the known-good previous binary (.old --> .exe).
- It permanently blacklists the problematic version in its local database so it
  will never attempt to download that build again.
- It relaunches the working app safely.

Downex heals itself automatically so you never have to troubleshoot.
