A download manager is only as convenient as its connection to your web browser.

If you have to manually copy links, open an app, paste the link, and click five
buttons every single time you want to save a file, you will quickly stop using it.

Downex features a native messaging bridge for modern browsers (Chrome, Edge,
Firefox, Brave):
- When you click a download link in your browser, the extension intercepts the
  URL in milliseconds.
- It passes the download request to the Downex core service via a local, secure
  loopback channel (downex_bridge.exe).
- Downex instantly displays its clean, lightweight confirmation dialog, letting
  you start the accelerated download with a single press of Enter.

No copy-pasting, no friction, no lost links.
