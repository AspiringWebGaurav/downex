Downloading at maximum speed is wonderful when you have the internet to yourself.
But what happens when someone else in your household is trying to stream a movie
or take an important voice call?

If a download manager takes 100% of your bandwidth, other devices experience
buffering and high ping lag.

To solve this, Downex includes precision speed limiting:
- In Settings, you can configure maximum download limits (e.g., 5 MB/s or 10 MB/s).
- Downex uses a token-bucket algorithm that releases network packets in smooth,
  micro-bursts rather than violent spikes.
- This keeps your download progressing steadily while leaving plenty of bandwidth
  free for video calls, gaming, and family members.

Great software lives in harmony with the people around you.
