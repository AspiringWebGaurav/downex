DOWNEX RESEARCH & STUDY ESSAY #25
Title: WHY WE COMBINED RUST AND REACT IN DOWNEX
Author & Architect: Gaurav Patil (https://gauravpatil.site)
Official Project: https://downex.apps.gauravpatil.site
================================================================================

Every programming language has its unique superpower:
- Rust is the gold standard for systems programming: it offers blistering C++-level
  speed, memory safety without garbage collection pauses, and rock-solid multi-
  threading.
- React and modern web standards provide the most flexible, expressive, and
  beautiful toolset for crafting responsive user interfaces.

Downex brings the best of both worlds together:
- The heavy lifting—multi-segment socket streaming, disk I/O, atomic file swapping,
  and cryptographic verification—is handled in pure Rust.
- The user interface—smooth animations, search filtering, dark/light themes, and
  interactive graphs—is rendered with modern React.

This architecture gives you the speed of a native C++ engine with the beauty of
a modern web interface, all while consuming a fraction of traditional memory.
