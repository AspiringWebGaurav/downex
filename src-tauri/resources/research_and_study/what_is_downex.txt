Title: WHAT IS DOWNEX AND WHY WAS IT BUILT?

At its heart, Downex is a modern, lightweight Windows Download Manager built
from the ground up for people who care about quality.

Downex takes the best ideas of traditional download accelerators—such as splitting
files into parallel pieces and automatically resuming interrupted transfers—and
rebuilds them using modern, memory-safe engineering.

Key pillars of the Downex philosophy:
1. Pure Speed: Saturate your available internet connection without stuttering.
2. Ultra-Light Memory: Run in the background using less than 15 MB of RAM.
3. Calm Interface: A clean, distraction-free design with both Light and Dark modes.
4. Total Respect: Zero popups, zero changelog interruptions, zero survey screens.

Downex isn't just a tool; it's a demonstration of how good desktop software can
feel when built with craftsmanship and care by Gaurav Patil.
