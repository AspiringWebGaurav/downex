Title: THE BEAUTY OF HARDWARE-SMOOTH 60 FPS PROGRESS BARS

Most progress bars in software feel clunky. They sit frozen at 12% for three
seconds, then jump violently to 34%, then freeze again. They feel digital,
rigid, and disconnected from reality.

In Downex, we believe visual feedback should feel analog, fluid, and alive.

The progress bar in the Downex updater is engineered like a game animation:
- It runs on a high-speed 16-millisecond timer (~60 to 120 FPS).
- Instead of jumping in chunky 1% integer blocks, it calculates smooth sub-pixel
  floating-point easing.
- The emerald green bar glides across the track like liquid, matching the native
  refresh rate of your monitor.

When software animations are smooth and continuous, they feel fast and calming
to watch.
