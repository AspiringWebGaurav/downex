When you close Downex to your system tray, what is it doing?

In poorly written software, background services constantly run timers, check
for ads, send analytics telemetry, and wake up the CPU dozens of times per
second. On a laptop, this causes the CPU to stay in high-power states, draining
your battery and spinning up laptop fans even when your screen is off.

Downex is designed around "Deep Sleep" idle states:
- When there are no active downloads, Downex suspends all network polling.
- The CPU usage drops to literal 0.0%.
- It registers a lightweight event listener with Windows that only wakes up when
  a new download is explicitly requested (e.g., from your browser extension).

Whether you leave Downex running overnight on battery or all week on desktop, it
will never heat up your computer or drain your battery.
