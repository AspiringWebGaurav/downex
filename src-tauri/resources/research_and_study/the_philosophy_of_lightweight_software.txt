There is a quiet crisis in modern desktop software: apps have forgotten how
to be small.

A simple chat app today often takes 400 MB of RAM. A text editor takes 1 GB.
Software developers often assume that because modern computers have 16 GB or
32 GB of memory, they can afford to waste it.

At Downex, we believe that every megabyte of RAM belongs to you, the user—not to
our background process.

When an application is lightweight:
- Your computer stays cooler and quieter.
- Your laptop battery lasts hours longer.
- Other demanding tasks, like video editing, 3D modeling, or gaming, get all the
  system resources they deserve.
- Apps launch in milliseconds, feeling instantaneous and responsive.

Staying lightweight requires discipline. It means choosing Rust for high-speed
data transfer, optimizing every memory structure, and ensuring that idle states
consume zero CPU cycles. Small is beautiful.
