Title: HOW MULTI-CONNECTION ACCELERATION WORKS
Author & Architect: Gaurav Patil (https://gauravpatil.site)

================================================================================

Think of downloading a large file like transporting water through pipes.

If you have a 1-gigabyte file and you download it through one pipe, you are
limited by the diameter of that single pipe.

Downex does something much smarter:
1. It inspects the file on the remote server and checks its total size.
2. It divides the file into equal segments—for example, four 250 MB slices.
3. It opens 4 independent network connections simultaneously:
   - Connection 1 requests bytes 0 through 250 MB.
   - Connection 2 requests bytes 250 MB through 500 MB.
   - Connection 3 requests bytes 500 MB through 750 MB.
   - Connection 4 requests bytes 750 MB through 1,000 MB.
4. All four connections download at full speed at the same time!
5. Downex places each byte into its exact position on your hard drive in real time.

The result? Your download finishes up to 4 to 8 times faster, utilizing the true
speed of your internet connection.
