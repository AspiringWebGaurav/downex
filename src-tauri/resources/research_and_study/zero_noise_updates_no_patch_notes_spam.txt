Title: ZERO-NOISE UPDATES: WHY WE HATE PATCH NOTES SPAM

Think about how modern operating systems and apps behave:
You open your laptop to get some work done, and suddenly you are greeted by three
different modal dialogs:
"Welcome to version 4.2.1! Look at our new buttons! Read our changelog!"

Why do apps do this? Because product managers want attention. But software is
supposed to be a tool, not a demanding pet. You don't need a refrigerator to give
you a speech every time it cools your groceries, and you don't need a download
manager to interrupt you just because a bug was fixed.

In Downex, we enforce the Zero-Noise Silent Rule:
- Background checks are 100% silent.
- When an update installs, it updates smoothly and quietly.
- There are ZERO modal popups, ZERO changelog spam screens, and ZERO forced reboots.

The only time Downex shows update status is right after an update finishes, where
it gently displays "UP TO DATE" for 3.5 seconds on the sidebar before fading back
to quiet idle. Peace of mind matters.
