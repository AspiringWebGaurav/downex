Title: SMART RETRIES AND HANDLING DROPPED WI-FI
Author & Architect: Gaurav Patil (https://gauravpatil.site)

================================================================================

Real-world internet connections are unpredictable. Microwaves cause Wi-Fi
interference, cellular hotspots fluctuate, and home routers occasionally restart.

When an ordinary app encounters a sudden network drop, it immediately panics and
marks the download as "Failed".

Downex implements an intelligent retry strategy called Exponential Backoff:
- When a packet drops, Downex doesn't immediately give up. It waits 1.5 seconds
  and attempts a gentle reconnect.
- If the network is still reconnecting, it waits 3 seconds, then 6 seconds,
  patiently giving your router time to restore the connection.
- It will retry multiple times in the background without bothering you with alert
  dialogs.

Once your Wi-Fi reconnects, Downex silently picks up where it left off, finishes
the download, and lets you know it's ready. Reliable software is patient software.
